# James Jenkins

James Jenkins is a CEO and COO-level business leader who helps enterprise-scale organisations build commercial advantage through the shift to agentic AI. His discipline is not advisory: he has held full P&L responsibility at Forsman & Bodenfors (Group CEO Sweden, UK, and Ireland from January 2024; European CEO 2025), B-Reel (Global COO, 8 years), and AKQA (12 years, London and San Francisco). His edge is human. He reads people, unites complex teams across agencies and clients, and connects that alignment directly to creative quality, operational performance, and revenue. As autonomous systems take over more decisions, the brands that win stay human at the core. He brings both: technology fluency and the ability to keep people and culture aligned around it. He is also co-founder of Encoded Brands.

## Positioning

The call comes when a board question, a competitive move, or a product decision makes the AI governance gap impossible to ignore. James works across four areas: technology and the agentic era; commercial leadership across markets; brand building and growth; and board leadership and governance. He is not a management consultant, a creative agency, or an AI strategy firm. He operates as a leader who has held the P&L, run the teams, and shipped the work. His own framing: "I don't think anyone has this figured out. I'm further into the question than most people I talk to, which is a different thing from having the answers." Culture cannot float free of performance. Strategy, creative quality, and commercial outcomes are connected, not treated separately.
